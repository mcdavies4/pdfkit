Who uses PDF annotation

Legal professionals

Lawyers and paralegals annotate contracts and briefs constantly — underlining key clauses, adding comments to flag issues, highlighting terms for discussion. Annotation is the standard way to mark up a document for review without altering the original text, which matters in legal contexts.

Students and academics

Highlighting key sentences in research papers, adding margin comments to reading material, circling important references. PDF annotation has largely replaced physical highlighting for academic reading — and unlike physical books, annotated PDFs are searchable.

Business and project teams

Reviewing proposals, marking up presentations, leaving feedback on reports before a meeting. Annotating a PDF and sending it back is faster than rewriting comments in an email and avoids the confusion of describing which section you mean.

Teachers and educators

Marking student work submitted as PDFs — adding comments, underlining errors, writing feedback. Annotated PDFs returned to students are clearer than emailed comments disconnected from the document.

Designers and creative professionals

Marking up design briefs, annotating wireframes, leaving feedback on layouts. Drawing tools let reviewers circle specific elements or draw arrows to the exact area they're commenting on.

Frequently asked questions

Can I annotate a PDF for free without Adobe?

Yes. RightPDFKit's Annotate PDF tool is completely free — no Adobe, no account, no upload. Works in any browser including Safari on iPhone.

Will my annotations show on other devices?

Yes. Annotations are embedded in the PDF in standard format and visible in any PDF reader — Adobe, Chrome, Preview, Foxit, and others.

Can I remove annotations later?

Yes. Annotations are a separate layer and can be removed by any PDF editor including RightPDFKit's Annotate tool — just clear your annotations before downloading.

What's the difference between annotating and editing a PDF?

Annotation adds highlights, comments and drawings on top of the PDF without changing the original content. Editing changes the actual text. Use Annotate for review and feedback; use Edit PDF Text to change the document content.

Can I annotate a scanned PDF?

Yes for drawing, text boxes and shapes — you can annotate anywhere on the page. Text highlighting requires a text layer. If your scanned PDF has no text layer, run OCR first then annotate.
