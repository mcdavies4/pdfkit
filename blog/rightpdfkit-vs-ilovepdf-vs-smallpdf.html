Privacy: the most important difference

iLovePDF and Smallpdf both upload your files to their servers. They delete files after a few hours, but your documents are transmitted over the internet and processed on external infrastructure. For sensitive documents — payslips, contracts, medical records, passport scans — this is a real risk.

RightPDFKit processes everything in your browser using WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device. This is a fundamental architectural difference, not just a privacy policy claim.

Speed

Because files don't need to upload and download, RightPDFKit is typically faster for operations like merge, split and compress — especially on larger files or slower internet connections. There's no waiting for a server to process and return your file.
