Page reordering methods

Drag and drop reordering

Click on any page thumbnail and drag it to the desired position. Visual drop zones show where the page will be placed. This intuitive method works perfectly for moving individual pages or small groups to new locations.

Reverse page order

Use the "Reverse Order" button to instantly flip your entire document from back to front. This is essential for documents that were scanned upside down or when you need the opposite page sequence.

Section-based reorganization

Select multiple consecutive pages and drag them as a group to move entire chapters, sections, or appendices to new locations within your document. Perfect for restructuring large documents efficiently.

Common page reordering scenarios

Fixing scanning errors

Documents fed through scanners sometimes get pages out of sequence. Quickly drag pages back to their correct positions to restore proper document order and readability.

Reorganizing presentations

Move slides around to improve presentation flow. Place your strongest points first, group related content together, or reorder sections based on audience feedback and presentation effectiveness.

Restructuring reports

Academic papers, business reports, and research documents often need section reordering during the editing process. Move executive summaries to the front, relocate appendices, or reorganize chapters for better logical flow.

Creating custom document versions

Generate different versions of the same document for different audiences by reordering sections. Technical details might go last for executives but first for engineers reviewing the same content.
