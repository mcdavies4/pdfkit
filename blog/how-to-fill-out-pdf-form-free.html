  1. Go to rightpdfkit.com and click Fill Form

What if the PDF isn't fillable?

Some PDFs look like forms but don't have interactive fields — they're just static documents with blank lines printed on them. Common examples: government forms saved as image PDFs, scanned paper forms, or PDFs created from Word documents without proper form fields.

If your PDF has no interactive fields, use the Annotate PDF tool instead:

  1. Open Annotate PDF
  2. Upload the form
  3. Select the Text tool and click anywhere on the page
  4. Type over the blank lines exactly where you want the text to appear
  5. Adjust font size to match the form's existing text
  6. Download the completed document

This works on any PDF regardless of whether it has form fields. It's the same approach used by accountants and legal teams who need to complete non-fillable forms without printing them.

The problem with most online PDF form fillers

Search "fill PDF form online" and you'll find Smallpdf, iLovePDF, Adobe, PDFFiller, DocuSign and dozens of others. They all upload your file to their servers.

For a rental application, a job application, a tax form, or a medical intake form — these contain your name, address, date of birth, national insurance number, salary, health information. Uploading this to a third-party server to fill in a few boxes is an unnecessary privacy risk.

RightPDFKit fills forms entirely inside your browser. The file stays on your device. There is no server that receives it. This isn't a marketing claim — it's architectural. Open DevTools Network tab while using it. You will see no file data transmitted during the fill process.

Adding a signature after filling

Most forms need both a completed form and a signature. Here's the full workflow:

  1. Fill the form fields using Fill Form and download the filled PDF
  2. Open the downloaded file in Sign PDF
  3. Draw your signature with mouse or finger, type it, or upload an image
  4. Drag the signature to the correct position on the page
  5. Download the signed, completed form

Both steps are free and run entirely in your browser. The final document has your filled fields and your signature, ready to email or upload.

Common PDF forms and tips for each

Government and tax forms

HMRC, DWP, council forms and most government PDFs are proper fillable forms with defined fields. Fill Form handles these directly. If a government form opens as a scanned image (common with older forms), use OCR first to extract the text, then use Annotate to overlay your answers.

Job applications

Most job application PDFs are fillable. If the employer has sent a locked PDF that won't let you type, unlock it first using the Unlock PDF tool, then fill the fields.

Tenancy and legal agreements

These often arrive as non-fillable PDFs with signature lines. Use Annotate to type your details on the blank lines, then Sign PDF to add your signature. If the document is long, use the page selector to navigate to the signature page.

Medical and insurance forms

These contain the most sensitive personal data. Filling them locally — without uploading to any server — is especially important here. RightPDFKit handles both fillable and non-fillable versions.

School and university forms

Consent forms, applications and enrolment documents are typically fillable PDFs. Fill Form completes these in seconds on any device including iPhone.
