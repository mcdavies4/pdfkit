Two types of PDF forms — and how to handle each

Not all PDF forms are built the same. Knowing the type you're dealing with tells you exactly which tool to use.

📋

Interactive forms (AcroForms)

These have real clickable fields — text boxes, checkboxes, dropdowns and radio buttons that highlight when you click them. Most government forms, application forms and contracts are built this way. Use Fill PDF Form in RightPDFKit — it reads all the fields and lets you type directly into them.

🖼

Flat forms (image-based)

These are scanned or printed forms saved as a flat image inside a PDF — no clickable fields at all. Common with older government documents, legacy contracts and photocopied forms. Use Edit PDF Text or Annotate in RightPDFKit to place text boxes exactly where the blanks are.

💡 Quick test: Try clicking on a blank in the form. If it highlights or shows a cursor inside a box — it's interactive. If nothing happens — it's flat and you'll need the text/annotate approach instead.

How to fill a flat PDF form (no clickable fields)

For scanned or image-based forms where clicking does nothing:

  1. Open RightPDFKit and go to Edit PDF Text
  2. Upload the form
  3. Click on the page near a blank line — a text cursor appears
  4. Type your text and drag it into position over the blank
  5. Repeat for each field
  6. Download the completed form

Alternatively, use the Annotate tool to add text boxes which let you position text more precisely over specific blanks.

How to fill and sign a PDF form

Most forms need a signature as well as typed data. The fastest workflow:

  1. Fill all fields using Fill PDF Form and download
  2. Open the downloaded file in Sign PDF
  3. Draw, type or upload your signature
  4. Drag it to the signature line and resize
  5. Download the signed, filled form

Filling PDF forms on iPhone and Android

RightPDFKit works on mobile — no app download needed:

  • iPhone: Open Safari → go to rightpdfkit.com → tap Fill PDF Form → upload from Files → tap each field to type using the keyboard → download

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I click on the fields in my PDF form?

The form is likely a flat image-based PDF rather than an interactive form. This happens when a form is scanned, photographed or printed to PDF without interactive fields. Use RightPDFKit's Edit PDF Text or Annotate tool to place text over the blank areas instead.

Can I save my progress and come back to it later?

RightPDFKit doesn't save in-progress work — download the partially filled form to your device whenever you need to step away, then re-upload it to continue.

Will the filled fields look different from the rest of the form?

Interactive fields match the form's designed font and size. For flat forms where you've added text manually, you can adjust the font, size and colour in the Edit Text tool to match the form's style.

Is it safe to fill sensitive forms like tax returns or medical forms?

Yes. RightPDFKit processes everything locally in your browser — your form data and personal information never leave your device. No server ever receives the file or its contents.

Can I fill PDF forms with checkboxes and dropdowns?

Yes. Interactive PDF forms with checkboxes, radio buttons and dropdown menus all work with RightPDFKit's Fill PDF Form tool — click to check, click to select from the dropdown.

How do I fill a PDF form on iPhone?

Open rightpdfkit.com in Safari, tap Fill PDF Form, upload your form from Files or Photos, tap each field and type using the iPhone keyboard, then tap Download to save the completed form.
