How to Extract Pages from a PDF Free — Keep Only What You Need

You have a 90-page PDF and need pages 4 to 6. Maybe it's a contract where the other side only needs the signature page, or a bank statement where the lender wants one month. Sending the whole file is slow, untidy and often shares more than you meant to.

Extracting pages means pulling the ones you want into a brand-new PDF and leaving the original untouched. RightPDFKit's Extract Pages tool does it in your browser: you pick pages on thumbnails or type ranges, and the new file is built on your own device.

Step by step

  1. Open rightpdfkit.com and choose Extract Pages.
  2. Drop in your PDF or click to browse. The tool shows how many pages it has.
  3. Select pages by clicking thumbnails, or type page numbers and ranges such as 1-3, 7, 10-12.
  4. Click Extract & Download.
  5. Open the new file and check the pages are the ones you wanted before you send it.

Extract, split or delete: which one do you need?

They sound alike but give different results. Extract builds a new PDF from the pages you choose. Splitting cuts a document into several files at once, for example one per chapter. Deleting removes the pages you don't want from the same file. If you know the pages to keep, extract. If you know the pages to lose, delete.

How page ranges work

Separate single pages with commas and give a span with a hyphen. 5 is page 5. 2-4 is pages 2, 3 and 4. 1, 3, 8-10 is pages 1, 3, 8, 9 and 10. Pages come out in order, so use Reorder Pages afterwards if you want a different sequence.

Common reasons to extract pages

  • Signature pages. Send only the page that needs signing, not the whole agreement.
  • One month of a statement. Share the period a landlord or lender asks for, and nothing else.
  • A chapter or table. Pull the section you need from a long report or manual.
  • Email size limits. A few pages are far smaller than the full file. If it is still large, compress it.
  • Studying. Keep just the lecture pages you need.

Why the order of operations matters for privacy

Extracting before you share is a simple form of data minimisation: people can't leak pages they never received. But extraction is not redaction. If a page you keep still contains a number or name you want hidden, redact it first, then extract.

Why not use an upload-based site?

Most popular page extractors send your file to their servers. That is a real risk for contracts, IDs and statements. In 2024 security researchers at Cybernews reported that two online PDF converters had misconfigured cloud storage that exposed passports, IDs, contracts and certificates uploaded by users. Because RightPDFKit extracts pages inside your browser, there is no upload to leak. You can check this yourself: open your browser's network tab, run an extraction and watch for any file leaving your device.

Does extracting reduce quality?

No. The selected pages are copied into the new PDF as they are, so text stays selectable, images keep their resolution and fonts are preserved. Only pages with interactive form fields or links that point to removed pages may behave differently.

Extracting pages on a phone

It works in mobile Safari and Chrome. Open the site, tap Extract Pages, choose the file from Files or your gallery, type the page range and download. For long documents, typing a range is easier than tapping small thumbnails.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract specific pages from a PDF for free?

Open RightPDFKit, choose Extract Pages, upload your PDF, select the pages by thumbnail or type ranges like 1-3, 7, 10-12, then click Extract and Download. It is free with no account.

Is my PDF uploaded when I extract pages?

No. The pages are copied into a new PDF inside your browser, so the file never leaves your device.

Can I extract pages that aren't next to each other?

Yes. Separate them with commas, for example 2, 5, 9-11, and they will be placed together in one new PDF.

Will the original PDF change?

No. Extraction creates a new file and leaves your original as it was.

Can I extract pages from a password-protected PDF?

If the PDF needs a password to open, unlock it first with the Unlock PDF tool, then extract the pages.

Is there a page limit or file size limit?

There is no limit set by RightPDFKit. Very large files depend on your device's memory, so a laptop will handle them better than an old phone.

What if my scanned PDF has no selectable text?

Extraction doesn't need selectable text, it copies whole pages. You can run OCR afterwards if you want the text to be searchable.
