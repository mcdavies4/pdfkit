Text vs image watermarks — which should you use?

📝 Text watermarks

Best for marking documents as CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, COPY, FOR REVIEW, or adding your company name. Crisp at any size, no image file needed, works on any background.

🖼 Image watermarks

Best for branding — add your company logo to every page. Use a PNG with a transparent background for the cleanest result. Ideal for proposals, reports and client deliverables.

Watermark settings explained

  • Opacity — how visible the watermark is. 20–40% is subtle, 60–80% is prominent.
  • Rotation — diagonal watermarks (45°) are traditional and harder to crop out. Horizontal (0°) looks cleaner.
  • Position — centre covers most of the page. Corner positions are less obtrusive.
  • Size — for text, 36–72pt works for most pages. For logos, 20–30% of page width is usually right.
  • Colour — grey or light colours for subtle marks. Red for urgent labels like DRAFT or VOID.
  • Pages — apply to all pages or specify a range (e.g. pages 2–10 only).

Common uses for PDF watermarks

🔒 Confidential documents

Mark contracts, financial statements, HR files and legal documents as CONFIDENTIAL before sharing. Makes unauthorised distribution traceable and clearly labelled.

📋 Draft versions

Add DRAFT to any PDF you're sharing for review but not yet finalising. Prevents the draft from being mistaken for the final approved version.

🏢 Brand watermarking

Add your company logo to proposals, reports and presentations before sending to clients. Keeps your brand visible even if the document is forwarded.

📸 Photography and creative work

Watermark proofs and sample images embedded in PDFs before sending to clients. Protects your work before payment is received.

✅ Copy control

Mark distributed copies with COPY, FOR REVIEW ONLY or a recipient's name to track distribution and discourage unauthorised sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a watermark to a scanned PDF?

Yes. RightPDFKit applies the watermark as a layer on top of each page — it works on scanned PDFs, image-based PDFs and text PDFs alike.

Can I watermark a password-protected PDF?

Unlock it first using RightPDFKit's Unlock PDF tool, then add your watermark. Both tools are free and process locally.

Will the watermark reduce the PDF quality?

No. The watermark is embedded as a vector or image layer — the underlying PDF content is not re-compressed or degraded.

Can I remove the watermark after adding it?

Watermarks added by RightPDFKit are embedded directly into the page content and are not easily removable. This is intentional — it makes them effective as security or branding marks.

Can I add a watermark on specific pages only?

Yes. In the watermark settings, choose which pages to apply it to — all pages, odd/even pages only, or a specific page range.
