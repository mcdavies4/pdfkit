How to delete pages from PDF — step by step

  1. Go to rightpdfkit.com and click Manage Pages
  2. Upload your PDF file from your device
  3. View all pages as thumbnails in the page management interface
  4. Click on individual pages to select them for deletion (selected pages show with a red border)
  5. Or use page ranges by typing numbers like "1-3, 7, 10-15" in the range selector

The page deletion happens instantly in your browser. You get a clean PDF with only the pages you want to keep, maintaining original quality and formatting for all remaining content.

Page selection methods

Individual page selection

Click on any page thumbnail to select it for deletion. Selected pages display with a red border and deletion icon. This method works perfectly for removing scattered unwanted pages throughout your document.

Page range deletion

Use the range selector to delete multiple consecutive pages quickly. Enter ranges like "1-5" to delete the first five pages, or combine ranges with individual pages like "1-3, 8, 12-15" for complex selections.

Bulk page management

For large documents, use the "Select All" option and then uncheck the pages you want to keep. This reverse selection method saves time when deleting most pages from a large PDF.

Common page deletion scenarios

Removing cover and blank pages

Reports and presentations often have cover pages or blank separator pages that aren't needed for digital distribution. Delete these to create cleaner, more focused documents that get straight to the content.

Extracting document sections

Large manuals or reports may contain multiple sections where you only need specific chapters. Delete unwanted sections to create focused documents for specific audiences or purposes.

Eliminating confidential content

Remove pages containing sensitive information before sharing documents externally. This ensures confidential data stays protected while allowing you to share the non-sensitive portions.

Creating document summaries

Extract executive summaries, key findings, or important charts by deleting everything except the most crucial pages. This creates focused overview documents for busy stakeholders.

Quality and formatting preservation

Original quality maintained

Page deletion doesn't affect the quality of remaining pages. Text stays crisp, images remain at original resolution, and vector graphics maintain their scalability throughout the deletion process.

Layout integrity

Remaining pages keep their original formatting, fonts, and styling. Page deletion simply removes unwanted pages without altering the content or appearance of pages you choose to keep.

Metadata preservation

Document metadata, bookmarks, and links on remaining pages stay intact. Only the deleted pages and their associated bookmarks are removed from the final document.

Professional document management

Version control

Create different versions of documents for different audiences by selectively removing pages. Marketing materials might need technical pages removed, while technical teams might not need marketing content.

File size optimization

Removing unnecessary pages reduces file size, making documents easier to email and faster to load. This is particularly valuable for large reports with many graphics or high-resolution images.

Compliance requirements

Some industries require removing specific types of content before document distribution. Page deletion helps ensure compliance with data protection and confidentiality requirements.
