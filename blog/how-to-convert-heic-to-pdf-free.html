Step by step

  3. Drag the thumbnails into the order you want the pages.

  5. Click Convert to PDF and download your file.

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the format iPhones and iPads save photos in by default. Apple switched to it with iOS 11 in 2017 because it keeps the same visual quality at roughly half the file size of JPG. That is great for phone storage, but many websites, government portals and older programs still don't accept it.

Why HEIC files won't open on Windows

Windows 10 and 11 can't open HEIC out of the box. You need Microsoft's HEIF Image Extensions from the Microsoft Store, and on some setups the paid HEVC Video Extensions as well. Chrome and Android browsers can't display HEIC either. RightPDFKit sidesteps all of this: when your browser can't read HEIC, the tool decodes it with its own built-in converter, so you don't need to install anything.

Combine several iPhone photos into one PDF

Multi-page documents are where this saves the most time. Photograph every page of a contract, tenancy agreement or set of payslips, drop all the HEIC files in at once, put them in order and export one PDF. If the result is too large to email, run it through Compress PDF.

Which page size and fit to choose

  • A4 or Letter for anything you will print or upload to a form. Orientation is picked automatically from the photo unless you choose one.
  • Match image makes each page the exact shape of the photo, with no white border. Good for sharing pictures.
  • Fit keeps the whole photo visible. Fill covers the page and may crop the edges.

Stop your iPhone saving HEIC (optional)

If you keep hitting this, you can make your iPhone save JPG instead: open Settings → Camera → Formats and choose Most Compatible. It only affects new photos, so existing HEIC files still need converting. The trade-off is that JPG photos take up more storage.

Why convert in your browser, not on a converter site

Photos of IDs, bank cards, payslips and signed documents are exactly the files you shouldn't hand to a random server. Most HEIC converters upload the image first. RightPDFKit decodes and builds the PDF on your own device, so the photo never leaves it. Read more in why you shouldn't upload PDFs online.

Converting on your iPhone itself

On an iPhone you can also open rightpdfkit.com in Safari, tap Image to PDF and choose photos straight from your library. Safari reads HEIC natively, so it is even faster there. You can also install the RightPDFKit Android app if you are on Android.
