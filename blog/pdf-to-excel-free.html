PDF to Excel vs copying and pasting

Manually copying tables from a PDF into Excel is tedious and error-prone — especially for multi-page tables or documents with dozens of rows. The converter extracts all tables at once, preserving row and column structure, so you don't have to reformat anything.

Common use cases

  • Financial statements and bank statements — import figures directly into your spreadsheet model
  • Invoices and purchase orders — extract line items for reconciliation
  • Research data and reports — pull tables into Excel for further analysis
  • Government data PDFs — census tables, budget documents, statistical releases

Why convert without uploading?

Financial documents contain sensitive data — account numbers, transaction history, personal details. Tools that upload your PDF to a server expose that data unnecessarily. RightPDFKit converts entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Nothing is ever sent to a server.
