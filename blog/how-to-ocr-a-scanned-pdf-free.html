How to OCR a Scanned PDF Free — Make Any Scan Searchable & Editable

A scanned PDF is just a picture of text — you can't search it, select a sentence, or copy a paragraph. OCR (Optical Character Recognition) fixes that by detecting the text in the image and embedding it as a real, selectable text layer underneath.

Most OCR tools either charge a subscription or require uploading your document — often a scanned ID, contract or medical record — to a remote server. RightPDFKit runs OCR entirely in your browser using Tesseract.js. Your file never leaves your device.

What OCR actually does

When you scan a paper document, the result is a flat image — even though it looks like text, the computer just sees pixels. OCR analyses those pixels, identifies letter shapes, and reconstructs the actual words. RightPDFKit then embeds that recognised text as an invisible layer positioned exactly over the original scanned image.

The visual result looks identical to the original scan — but underneath, the page now behaves like any other PDF: searchable with Ctrl+F, text is selectable, and you can copy and paste content out of it.

What OCR can and can't do well

  • Printed or typed text — excellent accuracy, typically 95%+ on clean scans
  • Tables — text is recognised but table structure may not be preserved perfectly
  • Handwriting — unreliable. OCR is built for printed text, not handwritten notes
  • Low-resolution or blurry scans — accuracy drops significantly below 200 DPI
  • Skewed or rotated pages — straighten the page first using RightPDFKit's Rotate tool for best results
  • Unusual fonts or stylised text — standard fonts (Arial, Times, Calibri) OCR best
⚠ Set expectations: No OCR engine — free or paid — achieves 100% accuracy on every document. Always proofread OCR output before relying on it for anything important, especially numbers, names and dates.

Tips for better OCR results

  1. Scan at 300 DPI or higher — low-resolution scans produce more recognition errors
  2. Straighten skewed pages — use the Rotate tool before running OCR if pages are tilted
  3. Good lighting and contrast — dark or uneven scans confuse character recognition
  4. Avoid handwriting — OCR is built for printed text; handwritten notes won't recognise reliably
  5. Proofread the result — always check OCR output, especially for names, numbers and dates

Frequently asked questions

How long does OCR take?

Processing time depends on document length and your device's processing power. A 10-page document typically takes under a minute since everything runs locally in your browser.

Does OCR work on multiple languages?

RightPDFKit's OCR is optimised for English text. Other languages may have reduced accuracy depending on the character set and font style used.

Will OCR change how my PDF looks?

No. The original scanned image stays exactly as it is — OCR simply adds an invisible text layer on top. Visually, the document is unchanged.

Can I OCR a photo of a document instead of a scan?

Yes, as long as the photo is reasonably clear and well-lit. Convert the photo to PDF first using RightPDFKit's Image to PDF tool, then run OCR on it.

Why is my OCR result full of errors?

Usually due to low scan resolution, skewed pages, poor contrast, or unusual fonts. Try re-scanning at a higher resolution with good lighting, or straighten the page first.
