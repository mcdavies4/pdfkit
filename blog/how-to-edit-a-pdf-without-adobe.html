What Adobe Acrobat does that free tools genuinely can't

To be fair — there are things Acrobat does that free tools don't match:

  • Certified digital signatures with cryptographic audit trails for legal and enterprise use
  • Advanced PDF form creation with conditional logic and calculations
  • Deep Microsoft 365 integration — edit PDFs directly inside Word and Teams
  • Cloud collaboration — share documents and track who viewed and edited them
  • Enterprise-grade redaction — scrubs metadata, not just visible content

For personal use and most small business workflows, none of these matter. For legal teams, regulated industries or enterprise IT — Acrobat's advantages are real. For everyone else, free tools cover 90%+ of what you actually need.
