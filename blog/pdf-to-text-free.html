Standard vs. OCR text extraction

Standard text extraction

For PDFs created from digital documents (Word, Google Docs, web pages), standard extraction reads the embedded text directly. This method is fast, preserves exact formatting, and maintains special characters perfectly.

OCR (Optical Character Recognition)

For scanned documents, photos of text, or image-based PDFs, OCR analyzes images to recognize and convert text. This technology works on handwritten text, printed documents, and mixed content with both text and images.

When to use each method

Use standard extraction for digital PDFs, reports, contracts, or any document created electronically. Use OCR extraction for scanned papers, photographed documents, or PDFs where standard extraction returns no text.

Extracting text from specific pages

Save time by extracting text from only the pages you need. Use the custom range field with these formats:

  • 1-5 — extracts text from pages 1 through 5
  • 3,7,12 — extracts text from pages 3, 7, and 12 only
  • 2-8,15 — extracts text from pages 2 through 8, plus page 15
  • 1,5-10,20 — extracts text from page 1, pages 5-10, and page 20

This is particularly useful for large documents where you only need specific sections, tables, or chapters as text.
