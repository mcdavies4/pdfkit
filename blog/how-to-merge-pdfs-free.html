How to Merge PDFs for Free — No Upload Needed

Need to combine multiple PDF files into one? You don't need to install software, create an account, or upload your files to any server. RightPDFKit merges PDFs entirely in your browser — your documents never leave your device.

How to merge PDFs — step by step

  1. Go to RightPDFKit and click Merge PDFs from the home screen.
  2. Add your files — drag and drop PDFs onto the upload zone, or click to browse. Add as many as you need.
  3. Reorder if needed — drag the file cards to set the order you want them combined.
  4. Click Merge PDFs & Download — your combined PDF downloads instantly.

Why merge PDFs without uploading?

Most online PDF tools send your files to their servers. That means your contracts, invoices, medical records or payslips are temporarily stored on someone else's computer. RightPDFKit uses WebAssembly to process everything locally — zero uploads, zero risk.

Common uses

  • Combining chapters of a report or thesis
  • Joining multiple invoices into one document for accounting
  • Merging bank statements for a mortgage application
  • Combining scanned pages from different sources into one file
  • Consolidating contracts or agreements into a single PDF

Can I merge more than 2 PDFs?

Yes — there's no limit on the number of files you can add. Add 20, 50, or more and they'll all be merged in one go. Drag to reorder before merging.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. RightPDFKit works on iPhone, Android, tablets and desktop. The interface is fully responsive. On mobile, tap the upload zone to select files from your phone storage or Google Drive.

Is merged PDF quality maintained?

Yes. The merge operation preserves all content — text, images, fonts, annotations, form fields and bookmarks — at full quality with no recompression.
