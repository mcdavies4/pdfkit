Is a free electronic signature legally valid?

For the vast majority of everyday documents — yes. In most jurisdictions, an electronic signature on a PDF carries the same legal weight as a handwritten one:

  • UK — valid under the Electronic Communications Act 2000 and eIDAS regulations
  • USA — valid under the ESIGN Act and UETA
  • EU — valid as a Simple Electronic Signature (SES) under eIDAS
  • Nigeria — valid under the Nigerian Evidence Act 2011
⚠ When you need more: Some documents — government filings, court documents, property transfers, certain financial agreements — require a Qualified Electronic Signature (QES) or a certified digital signature. For those, use DocuSign, Adobe Sign or a solicitor. For everyday contracts, offer letters, NDAs, consent forms and invoices, a drawn signature on a PDF is perfectly valid.
