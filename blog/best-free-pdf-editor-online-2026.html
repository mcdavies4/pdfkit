What to look for in a free PDF editor

  • Does it upload your file? For personal documents, contracts and medical records, local processing is far safer than uploading to an unknown server.
  • What are the actual free limits? "Free" often means 2–3 tasks per day, a 5MB file limit, or a 7-day trial. Check before you start.
  • How many tools does it include? Some editors do merge and compress but nothing else. Others cover 50+ tasks in one place.
  • Does it work on mobile? If you need to sign or annotate PDFs on your phone, make sure the tool is mobile-optimised.
  • Does it need an account? Creating an account just to compress a PDF is friction most people don't need.
