Step by step

  1. Open rightpdfkit.com and choose PDF to Excel.
  2. Drop in your PDF or click to browse.
  3. Check the detected table preview.
  4. Click Convert and download the .xlsx file.
  5. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers and tidy any merged cells.

When PDF to Excel works best

Text-based PDFs with clear rows and columns convert well: bank statements, invoices, price lists, timetables and exported reports. Scanned PDFs are images, so run OCR first to make the text selectable.

Tips for cleaner results

Convert one table-heavy section at a time by splitting the PDF first. Check totals after conversion, since numbers formatted with symbols may arrive as text. If a table spans several pages, merge the sheets in Excel afterwards.

Why local conversion matters

Financial documents contain account numbers and personal data. Because the conversion runs in your browser, nothing is sent to a server, which keeps you clear of third-party processing worries under GDPR.
