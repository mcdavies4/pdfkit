Scanned a document the wrong way up? Got a PDF where some pages are landscape and others are portrait? Rotating PDF pages takes seconds — no software needed, no upload required.

How to rotate PDF pages — step by step

  1. Open the Rotate PDF tool on RightPDFKit.
  2. Upload your PDF by dragging it in or clicking to browse.
  3. A thumbnail view of all pages appears. Click any page to select it, or use Select All to rotate every page at once.
  4. Choose your rotation: 90° clockwise, 90° counter-clockwise, or 180° (flip upside-down).
  5. Click Save & Download.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Click the thumbnail of the specific page you want to rotate, apply the rotation, and the rest of the document stays unchanged. This is useful when a scan has one portrait page in the middle of a landscape document.

Does rotation permanently fix the PDF?

Yes. The rotation is written into the PDF file — it's not just a view setting. When you download and open the file in any PDF reader (Adobe Reader, Preview, Chrome, your phone), the pages will be correctly oriented.

This is different from rotating a page in your browser's PDF viewer, which only adjusts the view temporarily and reverts when you close the tab.

Why do PDFs have rotated pages?

  • Phone or scanner orientation — photographing a document while holding your phone sideways
  • Mixed-orientation documents — a wide table scanned in landscape inserted into a portrait document
  • Incorrect export settings — some Word or LibreOffice exports flip the orientation
  • Merged PDFs — combining files that were created in different orientations

Rotate before sharing or uploading

If you're submitting a document to a government portal, HR system or university application, always check page orientation first. Upside-down pages can cause automatic document processing systems to fail or reject the upload entirely.
