How to convert PDF to PNG — step by step

  1. Go to rightpdfkit.com and click PDF to Images
  2. Upload your PDF file from your device
  3. Choose resolution: 2× (recommended) or 3× for ultra-high quality
  4. Select PNG format (preserves transparency and text sharpness)

Why choose PNG over JPG for PDF conversion?

Lossless image quality

PNG uses lossless compression, so text remains razor-sharp and diagrams stay crisp. JPG's lossy compression can blur text and create artifacts around sharp edges, making it unsuitable for documents with text or precise graphics.

Transparency support

PNG preserves transparent backgrounds from your PDF. If your document has transparent elements or you want to overlay the image on different backgrounds, PNG maintains those transparent areas perfectly.

Better for text and graphics

Documents with text, charts, diagrams, or line art look significantly better in PNG format. JPG is designed for photos and can make text appear fuzzy or introduce compression artifacts around sharp contrasts.

Professional quality output

PNG produces professional-quality results suitable for presentations, marketing materials, or print use. The file sizes are reasonable while maintaining excellent visual quality.

Converting specific pages only

Save time and storage by converting only the pages you need. Use the custom range field with these formats:

  • 1-5 — converts pages 1 through 5
  • 3,7,12 — converts pages 3, 7, and 12 only
  • 2-8,15 — converts pages 2 through 8, plus page 15
  • 1,5-10,20 — converts page 1, pages 5-10, and page 20

This is especially useful for large documents where you only need specific charts, diagrams, or summary pages as PNG images.
