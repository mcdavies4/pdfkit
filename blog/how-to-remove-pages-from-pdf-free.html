Why remove pages from a PDF locally?

When you upload a PDF to Smallpdf, iLovePDF or Adobe online tools, a copy of your document sits on their servers while it's processed — even if they delete it afterwards. For contracts, invoices, medical records, financial documents or anything confidential, that's a real risk.

RightPDFKit uses pdf-lib, a WebAssembly library that runs entirely inside your browser tab. The PDF bytes are never transmitted anywhere. You can even use it offline once the page has loaded.

Common use cases

  • Remove a cover page — strip the first page before sharing a report
  • Delete blank pages — clean up scanned documents with empty pages
  • Remove confidential sections — delete pages containing sensitive info before forwarding
  • Trim a document — remove appendices or back matter before printing
  • Extract specific pages — use the Extract tool to keep only the pages you need

Remove pages vs extract pages — what's the difference?

Delete Pages removes the pages you select and keeps everything else. Extract Pages does the opposite — it keeps only the pages you select and discards the rest. Both tools are free in RightPDFKit.

If you want pages 3–7 from a 20-page document, Extract is faster. If you want to delete just page 1 and page 20, Delete is the right tool.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove pages from a password-protected PDF?

You need to unlock it first. Use RightPDFKit's Unlock PDF tool to remove the password, then use Delete Pages. Both tools are free and process locally.

Is there a file size limit?

No. Because processing happens in your browser rather than on a server, there are no artificial file size limits. Performance depends on your device — very large PDFs (500+ pages) may take a few seconds longer.

Can I remove pages on iPhone or Android?

Yes. RightPDFKit works on mobile browsers including Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. Open rightpdfkit.com in your browser, tap Delete Pages, and select your file from Files or Photos.

Will the quality of my PDF change?

No. Removing pages does not re-compress or re-encode the PDF. The remaining pages are identical to the originals — same resolution, same fonts, same quality.

Can I undo after removing pages?

The original file on your device is never modified. RightPDFKit creates a new file when you click Download. To "undo", simply re-upload the original file.
